Privacy notice

This page says what information we hold, why, and what your rights are. It is written to match what this website and our service actually do — nothing here is boilerplate, and we collect very little.

Who we are

Keelson Holdings Ltd is the data controller for personal data collected through this website. You can reach us about anything on this page at info@shipitstudio.co.uk.

Visiting this website

This site has no sign-up forms, no accounts and no login. It sets no cookies. Three things happen in the background:

  • Hosting. The site is served by Vercel, whose servers keep standard request logs (your network address and the pages requested) for security and operations.
  • Analytics. We use Vercel Web Analytics, which counts page views in aggregate without cookies. We see how many people visited, not who you are.
  • Fonts. The typefaces load from Google Fonts, so your browser requests them from Google's servers, which see your network address in that request.

If you email us

Every button on this site opens your own email app — nothing is collected until you choose to write to us. If you do, we hold your email address and whatever you send in order to answer you and follow the conversation up. The lawful basis is legitimate interests: you wrote to us about the service. We do not add you to any mailing list, because we do not have one, and we will delete the correspondence if you ask.

When we qualify leads for a client

The service calls leads on behalf of client businesses. When we do that, the lead data — typically a name, phone number and the details of the enquiry — is supplied to us by the client, and it belongs to their relationship with their customer:

  • The client is the data controller for their leads; Keelson Holdings Ltd acts as a processor, on the client's documented instructions, under a written agreement.
  • We only call leads the client confirms have opted in to be contacted — the service does not make cold calls, and compliance with UK marketing rules (PECR) for the underlying consent sits with the client, which we verify before a campaign starts.
  • Calls may be recorded and transcribed to produce the qualification reports the client is paying for; where they are, callers are told in line with UK rules.
  • Lead data, recordings and transcripts are used only to run the client's campaign — never for our own marketing — and are returned or deleted in line with the client agreement when the engagement ends.

We use a small number of suppliers to run the service: hosting and cloud infrastructure, telephony and AI voice processing, and transactional email and messaging. Each acts as a sub-processor under contract, and we name the specific providers in each client agreement — or on request.

If our AI called you

That call happened because you enquired with one of our clients and agreed to be contacted — the AI will have said which business it was calling about. That business is the controller of your enquiry, so questions about your data are best sent to them, but you can equally write to us at info@shipitstudio.co.uk and we will either deal with it on their instructions or put you in touch. If you ask us to stop calling you, we suppress your number across our systems.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, object to our processing, and ask us to restrict it. Email info@shipitstudio.co.uk and we will respond within a month. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
