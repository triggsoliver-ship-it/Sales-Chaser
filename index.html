Questions

The things everyone asks.

Are these cold calls?

No. We only call leads that have already opted in — people who enquired and expect to hear from you. The AI can even confirm where they enquired at the start of the call. It's warm follow-up, done fast and consistently, and kept compliant with UK marketing rules.

Does it sound like a robot?

It sounds natural and conversational, follows your script, and handles questions. If a lead wants a human, it books them straight onto your team's calendar rather than forcing the conversation.

What counts as a "qualified" lead?

You define it — budget, timeframe, location, product fit, whatever matters to you. We only bill for leads that meet your criteria and agree to a call with your team.

How fast can we start?

Days. We build your calling agent from your script and qualification rules, you test it, then point your lead feed at us.

Does it work with our CRM?

Yes — qualified leads and call notes drop straight into your CRM or calendar in real time, so your team just turns up to booked calls.
