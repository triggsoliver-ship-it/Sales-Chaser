Questions buyers ask

Are these cold calls?

No. We only call leads that have already opted in: people who enquired and expect to hear from you. The AI can confirm where they enquired at the start of the call. It's prompt follow-up, done the same way every time and kept within UK marketing rules.

Does it sound like a robot?

It sounds natural, follows your script and handles questions. If a lead wants a person, it books them into your team's calendar instead of pushing on.

What counts as a qualified lead?

You decide: budget, timeframe, location, product fit, whatever matters to you. We only bill for leads that meet your criteria and agree to a call with your team.

How fast can we start?

Within days. We build your calling agent from your script and rules, you test it, then point your lead feed at us.

Does it work with our CRM?

Yes. Qualified leads and call notes go straight into your CRM or calendar as they happen, so your team just turns up to booked calls.
