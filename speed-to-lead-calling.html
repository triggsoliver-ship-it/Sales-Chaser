Why speed to lead matters

Someone who fills in a form has usually asked more than one firm. The first to ring gets the conversation while the buyer still has the tab open. An hour later they are back at work, and by the next morning they have often agreed a visit with someone else.

Why teams struggle to be fast

Leads arrive in the evening and at weekends, when nobody is at a desk. During the day your best people are on appointments. Dialling new leads is the job that always gets pushed to later.

What Sales Chaser does

  • Rings each new lead within a minute of the enquiry, day or night.
  • Says where the person enquired, so the call is expected.
  • Asks the questions you set: budget, timing, property, whatever decides a good lead.
  • Books interested buyers into your sales calendar.
  • Retries the ones who do not pick up, on a schedule you choose.

A worked example

An enquiry at 9:58pm

A homeowner asks for a quote at 9:58pm. Sales Chaser rings at 9:59pm, confirms they own the house and want the work done this year, and books a call with your surveyor for 12:30 the next day. Your team sees a booked appointment in the morning. This is an illustration, not a real customer.

How it works

  1. You send opted-in leads. People who have already asked to hear from you, plus your script and what a good lead looks like.
  2. The AI calls in seconds. Every new lead is rung within a minute, day or night. It follows your script, answers questions and checks the things you care about.
  3. Interested buyers go to your team. It books them straight into your sales team's calendar.

Common questions

How fast is the call?

Within a minute of the lead reaching us, at any hour you allow calls.

Are these cold calls?

No. We only call leads that have already opted in: people who enquired and expect to hear from you. The AI can confirm where they enquired at the start of the call.

What counts as a qualified lead?

You decide: budget, timeframe, location, product fit, whatever matters to you. We only bill for leads that meet it.

Does it sound like a robot?

It sounds natural, follows your script and handles questions. If a lead wants a person, it books them into your team's calendar.
